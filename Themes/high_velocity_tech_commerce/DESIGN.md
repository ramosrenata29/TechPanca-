## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for high-performance technology and hardware e-commerce. It channels the visceral energy, computational authority, and transactional urgency of the gamer and PC-enthusiast retail landscape.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate High-Contrast E-Commerce** with **Tactile Performance-Tuned Accents**. It relies on deep navy navigational architecture to signal technological reliability and scale, juxtaposed against incendiary high-visibility orange conversion triggers that guide consumer attention directly to purchase decisions.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Electrified Urgency:** Time-limited deals, flash discount ribbons, and countdown chronometers evoke action without visual chaos.
- **Hardware Precision:** Structural containment, clean data grids, and razor-sharp pricing logic provide clarity across spec-heavy component catalogs.
- **Trust & Clout:** Deep naval baselines provide an anchored institutional foundation, assuring authenticity and checkout security.

## Colors

The palette balances heavy institutional anchoring with hyper-converting focal points.

- **Primary (`#FF6500`):** Electric Circuit Orange. Reserved for high-value actions: main `COMPRAR` CTAs, lightning promotion banners, critical progress indicators, and active badges.
- **Secondary (`#003366`):** Deep Navy Core. Anchors the primary header, search chrome, category drawers, footer foundations, and enterprise credibility anchors. Sub-accents include deep midnight (`#0B1A30`) for header overlays and (`#002244`) for contrast bars.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A`):** Emerald Cash Advantage. Dedicated strictly to upfront settlement incentives: instant PIX pricing, cash discount callouts, and in-stock inventory indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` & `#111827`):** Graphite Titanium. Provides razor-sharp typographical legibility across specifications and titles.
- **Canvas Base (`#F2F4F7`):** Clean, cool industrial ground that makes product cards and white catalog surfaces pop cleanly without eye fatigue.

## Typography

Typographic scale is structured around numerical hierarchy and transactional dominance.

- **Weight Allocation:** Headlines and commercial triggers use `800` (ExtraBold) and `900` (Black) weights to establish assertive pricing cues. Technical descriptions, product specs, and system telemetry utilize `500` (Medium) and `400` (Regular).
- **Pricing Dual-Stack:** All commercial elements prioritize the instant cash price in `900` weight, followed directly by installment terms set in a secondary, muted weight (`600`) to highlight upfront discounts instantly.
- **Letter Spacing:** Heavy headlines feature tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to enhance cohesion on product cards and flash sale banners.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on a responsive 12-column desktop grid with strict lateral boundaries to maintain visual balance on wide monitors.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, max-width of 1360px centered, 24px gutters, and 40px outer margins. Supports 4 to 5 dense product cards per row.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px outer margins. Products reflow into 2 or 3 cards per row.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, 16px screen edge margins. Product catalog switches to a 2-column compact grid or continuous horizontal carousels with edge-bleed snapping.
- **Grid Cadence:** Vertical rhythms prioritize information density. Card interiors adhere to tight 8px-16px spacers to keep the primary CTA visible above the fold on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through high-contrast structural layering rather than heavy ambient blur.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Industrial off-white (`#F2F4F7`) providing crisp separation for white surfaces.
- **Layer 1 (Card Default):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a clean 1px structural outline in `#E5E7EB` and subtle ground settling: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 34, 68, 0.06)`.
- **Layer 2 (Card Hover & Flyouts):** Dynamic lift: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(0, 34, 68, 0.12)`, paired with an accent-tinted border (`rgba(255, 101, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Slide-in Carts, Floating Bars):** Sharp drop: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(11, 26, 48, 0.25)`.
- **Promotion Accent Elevation:** Lightning offer cards employ an internal, subtle orange ambient glow (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 101, 0, 0.3)`) to separate standard stock from promotional drops.

## Shapes

The design system maintains an intentional, engineered rigidity (`roundedness: 1`). Corner radii are restricted to soft, compact bevels:
- **Base Components:** 4px (`0.25rem`) standard curvature for buttons, inputs, product cards, and flash banners.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** 2px to 4px to maintain an industrial hardware feel; pill/circular rounding is avoided except for numerical quantity badges and avatar avatars.
- **Angle Accents:** Promotional deal headers and discount badges occasionally deploy a 12-degree chamfer/skewed cut to reinforce speed and competitive gaming aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Purchase ("COMPRAR"):** Full-width or wide footprint, solid `#FF6500`, bold uppercase text (`Montserrat 800`), 4px border radius. Interactive states: hover shifts to `#FF5000` with subtle translateY(-1px); active downscales to 0.98. Includes a fast-cart icon glyph on the leading edge.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent base, 1.5px solid `#003366`, dark navy text. Hover states fill to full navy with white typography.
- **Ghost Utility:** Clean text button with subtle icon (e.g., "Favoritos", "Comparar") in `#6B7280`, hovering to `#FF6500`.

### Product Cards
- Contained within a `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px `#E5E7EB` border.
- **Top Bar:** Houses the flash sale badge (e.g., `-25%`) and stock status indicator.
- **Center:** High-fidelity hardware render on pure white canvas with an aspect-ratio of 1:1.
- **Pricing Stack:**
  - Original crossed-out price (`#9CA3AF`, 12px, line-through).
  - Prominent cash/PIX price in `#16A34A` or `#FF6500` (22px-26px, weight 900).
  - Installment breakdown in `#4B5563` (e.g., "10x de R$ 99,90 sem juros").
- **Action Footprint:** Sticky bottom container hosting the high-visibility "COMPRAR" button.

### Badges & Flash Deals
- **Lightning Tag:** Solid `#FF6500` background with contrasting `#FFFFFF` text, prefixed with a stylized bolt icon.
- **Instant PIX Pill:** Soft emerald tint (`#DCFCE7`) with deep emerald copy (`#15803D`) and bold "À VISTA NO PIX" label.
- **Countdown Chronometer:** High-contrast bar placed over promotional hero cards. Deep navy background (`#0B1A30`), displaying segmented hours, minutes, and seconds alongside a dynamic orange capacity indicator (showing remaining deal units).

### Inputs & Coupon Elements
- **Search Header Input:** Integrated into the `#003366` header bar. High-contrast white input field, 40px–48px height, 4px border radius, featuring an electric orange search action button docked right.
- **Coupon Module:** Dashed border container (`#D1D5DB`) with dual-segment field: monospaced coupon input paired directly with an attached, bold "APLICAR" button.

### Category Menus
- **Mega Menu Dropdown:** Full-width under-header drawer with a deep navy sidebar listing main tech categories (Hardware, Periféricos, Gamer, Computadores), opening into dense sub-category link columns on clean white elevation.
